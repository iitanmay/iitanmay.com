Work

  • 2026.01 - Present

    United Kingdom

    Postdoctoral Research Fellow
    University of Oxford
    Working with Prof. Thomas J. Aubry on the NERC-funded PHOENIX project, investigating buoyant multiphase plumes in stratified and sheared environments using laboratory experiments, integral modelling and large-eddy simulations.
    • Developing an analogue flow facility for laboratory studies of buoyant plumes in stratified crossflow.
    • Developing integral plume models and large-eddy simulation datasets to investigate plume dynamics, transport and entrainment under ambient stratification and velocity shear.
  • 2021 - 2025

    India

    Doctoral Researcher
    Indian Institute of Technology Delhi
    Investigated transport, mixing and entrainment in turbulent jets, fountains and air curtains using laboratory experiments, large-eddy simulations and Lagrangian transport analysis.
    • Developed experimental and numerical frameworks for studying turbulent impinging fountains and air curtains.
    • Investigated the physical mechanisms governing infiltration and transport across air curtains using Eulerian and Lagrangian analyses.
  • 2017 - 2020

    Australia

    Graduate Researcher
    The University of Melbourne
    Investigated mixing in stratified gravity currents using high-resolution molecular tagging velocimetry and thermometry.
    • Performed simultaneous velocity and temperature measurements to quantify mixing in lock-exchange flows.
    • The resulting first-author Physics of Fluids article was selected as an Editor's Pick.
  • 2014 - 2016

    Taiwan

    Graduate Researcher
    National Tsing Hua University
    Developed computational methods for incompressible fluid-flow simulation, including multigrid and GPU-based lattice Boltzmann solvers.
    • Developed an in-house multigrid solver for the unsteady incompressible Navier-Stokes equations.
    • Implemented GPU-accelerated lattice Boltzmann solvers and immersed-boundary methods for fluid-solid interaction problems.
