New year, new beginnings! 2026 marks me joining University of Oxford to work on the NERC-funded PHOENIX project ("Influence of compleX sOurce and wiNd conditions on Eruptive Plume Height").

This project aims to transform our understanding of how complex source geometry and vertically varying atmospheric winds impact volcanic plume heights. This is critical because current models often fail to accurately simulate complex real-world plumes, resulting in a limited understanding of volcanic hazards and climate impacts.

To address these challenges, we will use a multidisciplinary approach combining:

- **Observational data** from field investigations at sites like Campi Flegrei.
- **Small-scale laboratory experiments** reproducing analogue volcanic plumes in a controlled laboratory setup. This is where I come in.
- **3D numerical simulations** to improve existing 1D plume models.

I look forward to working with Prof. Thomas Aubry and our UK and international partners to improve volcanic response protocols and climate projections.
